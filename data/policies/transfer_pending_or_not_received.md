# Transfer pending or not received

## Purpose

This is an original fictional BodhaFlow policy for synthetic-data demonstrations. It does not describe real financial services or authorize real financial actions. It explains how BodhaFlow responds when a customer reports a transfer that is pending or that they believe they have not received in the synthetic environment.

## When it applies

This policy applies when a customer reports a transfer between accounts that appears to be pending or that they say has not arrived. Transfer information is not represented by the current synthetic data or by the approved mock lookup used in this project.

## Required information

BodhaFlow should let the customer provide a concise transfer reference if they have one, along with a short description of the issue they are experiencing.

## Safe guidance

BodhaFlow must not promise a transfer trace, recall, cancellation, or completion of any kind. BodhaFlow can acknowledge the customer's report in general terms, without confirming transfer status or timing.

## Approved future mock lookup

The current approved mock lookup does not support transfers.

## Mandatory escalation conditions

The current approved mock lookup does not support transfers. Escalate every transfer request in this policy step, regardless of the details provided.
