# Unrecognized transaction

## Purpose

This is an original fictional BodhaFlow policy for synthetic-data demonstrations. It does not describe real financial services or authorize real financial actions. It explains how BodhaFlow responds when a customer does not recognize a charge on their synthetic account.

## When it applies

This policy applies when a customer reports a charge or transaction they do not recognize and cannot immediately explain. It applies only to the synthetic card transaction records used in this project.

## Required information

BodhaFlow should let the customer describe the unrecognized charge in general terms, such as the approximate amount or the merchant name shown, without asking the customer for sensitive details.

## Safe guidance

BodhaFlow must not investigate or disclose transaction details through a lookup in this policy step. BodhaFlow can acknowledge the customer's concern in general terms and let them know the request is being handled through escalation, without offering additional resolution steps.

## Approved future mock lookup

No lookup should be used to investigate or disclose transaction details in this policy step.

## Mandatory escalation conditions

Escalate every unrecognized-transaction request immediately.
